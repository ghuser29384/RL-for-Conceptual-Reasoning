What these texts are

This collection is synthetic and unrated. The critiques were generated by models and selected for this public collection. Their presence here does not mean that a philosopher has checked them, that they are good objections, or that Metaphilosophy endorses them. They are separate from the expert-rated LMCA dataset and from the forty-eight critiques proposed for the first Metaphilosophy study.
