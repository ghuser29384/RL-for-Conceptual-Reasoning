## Early-career public opportunity notice

**Title:** Early-career philosophy researchers: expert-rater Pilot 01 expression of interest

> Metaphilosophy is recruiting advanced philosophy doctoral students, postdocs, recent PhDs, and directly relevant early-career researchers for a small expert-rating pilot.
>
> Pilot 01 contains 48 public critiques across 12 philosophical domains. Every critique will receive two independent blind ratings using a structured seven-dimension rubric. Selected raters are likely to receive approximately 12–24 ratings over a maximum four-week window, after completing a separate human-reviewed calibration.
>
> The current USD 400 core-rater budget is a limited pooled volunteer honorarium distributed proportionally by accepted rating units. It is not a wage, and no applicant is guaranteed selection, assignments, or an individual amount.
>
> This is a public development pilot, not a hidden benchmark. Results will be used to measure rating time, agreement, disagreement causes, adjudication demand, and operational reliability before any larger programme.
>
> Expression of interest: https://www.metaphilosophy.org/pilot-raters/
