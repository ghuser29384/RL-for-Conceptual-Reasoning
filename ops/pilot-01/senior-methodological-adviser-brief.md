# Pilot 01 senior methodological adviser brief

## Purpose

Senior researchers are not being asked to perform bulk annotation. Their role is to review the methodology at the points where prior experience with LMCA, philosophical argument evaluation, and expert disagreement has the highest marginal value.

The request is deliberately bounded to approximately **one to three hours per adviser**. Any additional work requires a separate agreement.

## Requested contribution

A senior methodological adviser may be asked to:

1. Review the concise Pilot 01 protocol and Rubric v2.
2. Review **8–12 shared public calibration cases**.
3. Identify ambiguous instructions, foreseeable rating confounds, or missing safeguards.
4. Comment on the proposed disagreement and adjudication triggers.
5. Review the deidentified pilot summary after completion.
6. Recommend qualified early-career raters and alternates.
7. Where applicable, advise on access to the canonical row-level LMCA data and its license or redistribution terms.

An adviser may optionally agree to review **up to ten difficult disagreement cases**. This is not assumed and is not part of the initial request.

## Explicit exclusions

The initial request does not ask a senior adviser to:

- complete a 133–134 rating workload;
- serve as a general project manager;
- endorse Metaphilosophy publicly;
- permit use of their name or comments without explicit permission;
- disclose private contact information for other researchers;
- perform unpaid work beyond the stated cap;
- adjudicate the full future 400-critique Hard Set.

## Pilot being reviewed

Pilot 01 uses 12 public synthetic positions with four critiques each: **48 critiques and 96 blind initial ratings**. It is a public development and workflow pilot, not a hidden benchmark. The pilot is intended to measure actual rating time, agreement, disagreement causes, adjudication demand, attrition, and operational reliability before any full-set expansion.

## Honoraria boundary

The current USD 500 pilot budget is a limited volunteer-honoraria pool: USD 400 for accepted early-career rating units and USD 100 reserved for accepted required adjudication units. General methodological advice does not automatically earn a payment unit under the current rules. An adviser may decline any honorarium or discuss a separate arrangement if external funding is obtained.

## Confidentiality and attribution

Pilot items are public. Protected Hard Set items must not be shown. Adviser feedback is private by default. Metaphilosophy may quote, summarize, or publicly name an adviser only after recording explicit permission for the exact use.

## Completion record

For each adviser, record:

- invitation and response date;
- accepted scope and time cap;
- conflicts and prior exposure;
- materials reviewed;
- feedback received;
- permission status for attribution or quotation;
- whether follow-up was requested.
