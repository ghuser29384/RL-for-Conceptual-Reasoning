# Pilot 01 early-career expert-rater recruitment plan

## Objective

Activate **4–8 qualified early-career core raters**, targeting six, plus two prequalified alternates. Every one of the 48 public pilot critiques must receive two independent blind ratings.

## Candidate profile

Priority candidates are:

- advanced philosophy doctoral students;
- philosophy postdoctoral researchers;
- recent philosophy PhDs;
- early-career researchers in decision theory, AI safety, epistemology, ethics, philosophy of mind, political philosophy, logic, or adjacent fields;
- researchers with equivalent demonstrated experience evaluating complex arguments.

Prestige and affiliation are not substitutes for calibration. Every activated rater must pass the same human-reviewed qualification and calibration gates.

## Recruitment sequence

### Channel 1 — adviser referrals

Ask each senior methodological adviser to recommend two or three early-career candidates. A referral creates a candidate record, not qualification or a commitment.

### Channel 2 — public expression of interest

Publish the non-binding application at `/pilot-raters/`. The form asks only for background, relevant experience, availability, conflicts, and consent. It does not collect bank credentials, tax identifiers, identity documents, or payment-account details.

### Channel 3 — targeted public-profile outreach

After adviser referrals are processed, identify candidates from public university, research-institute, and personal profiles. Contact only candidates whose stated work is directly relevant. Do not infer private contact details or use Google Contacts.

### Channel 4 — public opportunity notices

Prepare notices for relevant philosophy and AI-safety communities. Publish only after the direct senior-adviser email plan is approved and the expression-of-interest route is live. Each notice must link to the same terms and may not promise an individual payment amount.

## Application stages

1. **Expression of interest:** background, expertise, relevant experience, availability, profile link, conflicts, and consent.
2. **Human eligibility screen:** verify relevant training or equivalent work, exclude obvious duplicates and conflicts, and record reasons.
3. **Calibration:** complete the frozen public calibration without external AI assistance.
4. **Human qualification decision:** qualified, reserve, needs clarification, or not qualified.
5. **Capacity confirmation:** accept an exact proposed assignment range and four-week window.
6. **Payment/legal onboarding:** only for activated contributors; collect sensitive payment or tax information through an appropriate secure process, not ordinary email.
7. **Assignment:** blind allocation after readiness passes.

## Workload and honorarium disclosure

- Pilot size: 48 critiques and 96 required initial ratings.
- Expected individual tranche: approximately 12–24 ratings, depending on the activated panel size.
- Current budget: USD 400 pooled core-rater honoraria, distributed pro rata by accepted eligible rating units.
- This is a limited volunteer honorarium, not a wage or full-cost estimate.
- No applicant is guaranteed selection, assignments, or an individual payment amount.

## Selection controls

- Maintain domain coverage rather than selecting only one philosophical subfield.
- Do not activate two raters where a known relationship or shared authorship creates a material independence concern for the same assigned item.
- Preserve the source and authorship blinding available in the public release.
- Record prior item exposure and exclude a rater from any case where it creates a material conflict.
- Keep at least two qualified alternates before the pilot clock begins.
- Use applicant IDs, not names, in assignment-generation and quality-analysis files.

## Recruitment metrics

Track:

- applications by source;
- eligible and ineligible counts with reasons;
- calibration completion and pass rates;
- accepted capacity;
- domain coverage;
- time from application to decision;
- withdrawals before launch;
- alternate coverage;
- outreach response rate.

## Email boundary

No email is sent by this plan. Before sending any adviser, referral, candidate, departmental, or community email, present the exact recipient groups, message purpose, and follow-up schedule to Ellen Sun for approval. Draft creation and revision are allowed; sending requires explicit authorization.
