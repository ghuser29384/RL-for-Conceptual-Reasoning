# Internal draft — Metaphilosophy pilot outreach plan

**No email has been sent.**  
**No email may be sent until the project owner reviews and approves the exact recipients, messages, attachments, sender, follow-up rule, and reply-handling plan. Role-specific readiness requirements below also apply.**

## Outreach objective

Recruit and confirm:

- 6 early-career core raters;
- 2 dedicated adjudicators;
- at least 2 prequalified rater/adjudicator replacements; and
- 2–4 senior methodological advisers for a bounded protocol review.

The senior-adviser and bulk-rater roles are intentionally separate. Senior researchers are not asked to rate the 48 critiques or perform routine adjudication.

## Role-specific preconditions

A single undifferentiated outreach gate would create the wrong order: methodological feedback is needed before the final protocol, item set, calibration rule, and participant offer can be frozen. Adviser outreach and participant outreach therefore have separate preconditions.

### Before methodological-adviser outreach

Do not contact an adviser until the project owner has approved:

1. **Q-006A**, including the consultation design and the fact that all numerical thresholds and exact items remain provisional;
2. the bounded adviser brief, LMCA audit, balanced methodology recommendation, public example, and response form;
3. the exact adviser recipient list, order, public affiliation, role rationale, and public source for each professional address;
4. the exact individualized message and single follow-up;
5. the authorized sender and reply-handling owner; and
6. confirmation that the packet contains no protected item, private participant record, payment promise, or endorsement claim.

Payment, tax, sanctions, and participant-jurisdiction readiness are **not** prerequisites for an uncompensated bounded methodological review. If any adviser compensation, reimbursement, or other consideration is proposed, its budget, payment, legal, and tax implications require separate approval before that offer is made.

Approval of Q-006A alone does not authorize sending. The project owner separately approves the complete adviser email packet.

### Before early-career rater or adjudicator outreach

Do not contact prospective raters or adjudicators until the project owner has approved:

1. the relevant Q-006A design and recorded the first methodological feedback;
2. either Q-006B or a narrowly defined expression-of-interest protocol that clearly states which methodological and item decisions remain provisional;
3. the proposed 16 protected production ratings plus 8 shared public calibration ratings;
4. the honest workload disclosure, including the 3–8 hour core-rater planning envelope and the fact that adjudicator workload remains trigger-dependent;
5. the honoraria pools, zero-unit calibration rule, and a feasible payment path for the recipient jurisdictions likely to be contacted;
6. the blindness, conflict, source/provenance, balanced-assignment, withdrawal, privacy, and data-retention terms;
7. the exact recipient list, role classification, message, attachments, and single follow-up;
8. the private method for collecting later consent, identity, tax, payment, and sanctions-screening data; and
9. the authorized sender and reply-handling owner.

A first expression-of-interest email need not collect tax or identity documents. It must not imply that selection, work, or payment is guaranteed. No candidate may begin calibration or production until Q-006B, payment/legal readiness, qualification, consent, conflict/exposure review, and explicit acceptance are complete.

Public recruitment remains closed unless the project owner separately approves a public campaign after the same disclosures and data-collection controls are ready.

## Recommended sequence

### Stage 1 — methodological advisers

After Q-006A and separate email-packet authorization, contact two senior researchers in the first wave and hold two additional candidates in reserve. Each message is individual, not a bulk mailing.

The request is limited to either:

- approximately 20 minutes of asynchronous review; or
- one 30-minute call.

The email attaches or links only to:

- the bounded adviser brief;
- the human-readable pilot protocol;
- the LMCA-to-pilot methodology audit;
- the balanced assignment, calibration, and analysis recommendation;
- the seven-dimensional rubric;
- one public, non-protected example; and
- the three-question response form.

It explicitly says:

- the existing LMCA work is prior art;
- Metaphilosophy is not claiming the first conceptual-argument rating dataset;
- no bulk rating or ongoing board role is requested;
- no endorsement is implied; and
- comments will not be attributed without permission.

Recommended follow-up rule: one concise follow-up after five business days, then close the request unless the recipient reopens it.

### Stage 2 — early-career core raters and replacements

After incorporating or recording the first methodological feedback and passing the participant-outreach preconditions, contact an initial wave of 8–10 early-career candidates for expressions of interest. State clearly that interest is not acceptance and no work should begin.

If fewer than six qualified and available candidates plus two replacements are identified, contact a second wave of 4–8 candidates. Do not over-recruit people into unpaid calibration or imply that every interested person will receive an assignment.

The first-contact message includes:

- the role brief;
- the proposed 16 protected production ratings plus 8 shared public calibration ratings;
- the eventual 28-day production-window rule;
- the LMCA source estimate of 5–15 minutes per short rating;
- the resulting estimate of roughly 2–6 hours of scoring alone and the broader **3–8 hour planning envelope**, with additional time possible for assigned re-ratings;
- the fact that calibration is not an honorarium contribution unit under the current plan;
- the USD 400 contribution-weighted core-rater pool and an equal-production-work illustration;
- the fact that the amount is limited honoraria, not a wage or full-cost compensation;
- the blindness, conflict, provenance, balanced-assignment, and withdrawal rules;
- the fact that exact dates do not exist until readiness; and
- a request only for interest, relevant background, jurisdiction, feasible payment rail, and potential availability.

Do not request bank, tax, passport, government-ID, or sanctions-screening documents in the initial email or through an unapproved public form.

Recommended follow-up rule: one concise follow-up after five business days. No repeated pressure.

### Stage 3 — adjudicators

Adjudicator invitations are separate from core-rater invitations. A candidate may express interest in both roles, but the final panel preserves role separation for the pilot.

The adjudicator message explains:

- the shared eight-item public calibration proposal;
- that workload depends on pre-registered disagreement, low-clarity, unresolved-verification, and integrity triggers;
- that both adjudicators independently review required cases before seeing each other's disposition;
- that consensus is not forced;
- that original ratings are preserved;
- that the USD 100 reserve is contribution-weighted; and
- that unused reserve funds remain unspent.

The adjudicator workload cannot be represented as a fixed number of hours until the final trigger policy and pilot items are frozen. Candidate acceptance requires a workload range or capacity cap derived from pre-launch simulations or screening estimates.

At least one prequalified replacement should be available for each role family if feasible.

## Recipient selection principles

Use public professional information and direct institutional or personal professional addresses. Do not use Google Contacts to build the list.

Prefer candidates who collectively provide:

- coverage across the six proposed topic families;
- demonstrated object-level philosophical reasoning;
- experience with teaching, grading, peer review, argument mapping, debate, or research evaluation;
- methodological diversity;
- no protected-item exposure or authorship conflict; and
- feasible participation in calibration and the eventual four-week production window.

Do not select solely by institutional prestige, social proximity, effective-altruism affiliation, or agreement with the project's philosophical premises.

## Email plan for project-owner approval

Before each outreach stage, provide the project owner with one review packet containing:

1. the exact recipient list, order, public affiliation, role rationale, and address source;
2. the complete individualized message and attachments;
3. the one permitted follow-up;
4. the sender address and reply-handling plan; and
5. a statement that no protected items, labels, private reviewer data, unsupported novelty claim, uncommitted payment claim, or endorsement claim are included.

For participant outreach, the packet additionally includes the role-specific workload, honoraria, calibration, privacy, jurisdiction, and payment-feasibility disclosures.

The project owner approves or edits the packet before Gmail or any other sending tool is used.

## Reply handling

Classify replies into:

- interested — information complete;
- interested — missing non-sensitive information;
- requests clarification;
- declines;
- no response; or
- conflict/exposure concern.

Do not infer consent or availability from silence. Do not add a person to the confirmed panel until qualification, calibration, conflict/exposure review, payment/legal readiness, and explicit acceptance are recorded.

## Audit record

For each authorized message, record:

- recipient and role;
- public source for the address;
- owner approval reference;
- exact message version;
- sent timestamp;
- follow-up timestamp, if any;
- reply classification;
- consent and role status; and
- any conflict, exposure, or data-deletion request.

The audit log must not be committed publicly if it contains personal email addresses or sensitive participant records.
