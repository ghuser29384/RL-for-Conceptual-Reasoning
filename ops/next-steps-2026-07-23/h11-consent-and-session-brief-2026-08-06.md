# H-11 synthetic usability session — consent and participant brief

**Draft ID:** `H11-CONSENT-BRIEF-2026-08-06-V1`
**Status:** draft for owner and compliance review; not final; not sent
**Exercise type:** private synthetic product and rubric usability verification

## Purpose

Metaphilosophy is testing whether a qualified person can understand and use a protected expert-rating workflow adapted from the method in *A dataset of rated conceptual arguments*.

This session is quality assurance. It is not a request for a research rating, is not part of the 48-critique pilot, and does not test whether the participant agrees with any philosophical conclusion.

## What the session involves

The participant will:

- open a single-use protected invitation;
- review one explicitly synthetic position and four synthetic sibling critiques;
- consult the full seven-dimensional rubric;
- enter synthetic ratings, a rationale, confidence, interpretation information, and other required fields;
- save, close, reopen, and resume;
- encounter one controlled recovery or failure condition;
- submit and receive a receipt;
- use either a correction-request or withdrawal-request path; and
- complete a brief comprehension and usability debrief.

Expected time is 60–90 minutes.

## Methodological concepts tested

The debrief checks whether the interface communicates:

- the distinction between centrality and strength;
- why `strength × centrality` is the substantive-impact quantity;
- why low clarity makes other component ratings less reliable;
- why initial ratings are independent and immutable;
- why source and peer information are hidden; and
- how disagreement may remain unresolved rather than being forced into consensus.

## Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. The person may decline before starting or stop during the session without reputational or research consequences.

Stopping does not authorize public attribution, quotation, or use of incomplete synthetic scores.

If the participant has started and a platform defect, security stop, or operator-required termination prevents completion, the participant receives the full USD 75. A voluntary stop unrelated to a product defect does not automatically earn the full amount; any exception must be documented in writing.

## Data generated

The private H-11 record may contain:

- identity and invitation-administration records;
- session and access-security events;
- draft and submitted synthetic fields;
- timestamps and submission receipts;
- correction or withdrawal events;
- technical error and retry events;
- operator incident notes;
- de-identified usability notes; and
- the participant’s comprehension answers.

The record must not contain bank credentials, tax-identification numbers, government identity documents, or unrelated personal information.

## Research-use boundary

None of the session’s scores, rationales, technical events, or usability observations may be:

- added to the Metaphilosophy research dataset;
- used to score or train a model;
- treated as evidence about the participant’s philosophical judgment;
- included in a publication or funding claim; or
- made public without a separate, specific permission.

The workflow must retain the machine-readable flags `synthetic_rehearsal_only` and `research_ratings_authorized=false`.

## Privacy and confidentiality

- No other dry-run participant should see the person’s identity, ratings, drafts, debrief, or completion status.
- Public attribution or quotation requires separate consent.
- Default recording policy is **no audio and no video**.
- Screen recording, audio recording, or identifiable screenshots require separate explicit consent.
- Identifiable synthetic-session records are retained until 90 days after H-12 sign-off or final defect closure, whichever is later.
- After that point, direct identifiers are deleted; only de-identified usability findings, integrity hashes, and the minimum audit summary are retained.
- Technical logs and de-identified notes are used only for internal workflow verification and defect resolution.

## Security

Access is single-use, expiring, revocable, and replaceable. The participant should report a lost or forwarded link immediately.

The participant should not send credentials, tax forms, banking details, or identity documents through ordinary email.

## Risks and stop rule

Foreseeable burdens include time, confusion, accidental disclosure, and loss of draft work. The operator must stop the session for any plausible P0 or P1 security, privacy, comprehension, or data-integrity defect.

After a stop:

1. revoke outstanding access;
2. preserve the private audit record;
3. do not ask the participant to retry until the defect is corrected and the exact release is reverified;
4. disclose what was retained and what will happen next; and
5. apply the owner-approved forced-stop compensation rule.
