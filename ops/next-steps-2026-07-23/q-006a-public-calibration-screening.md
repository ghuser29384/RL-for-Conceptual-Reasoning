# Q-006A public calibration-candidate screening

**Status:** Five exposed LMCA examples screened; zero selected.  
**Authorization boundary:** Public, non-protected screening only. No calibration-material freeze, participant work, outreach, or rating is authorized.  
**Source role:** *A dataset of rated conceptual arguments* is methodological prior art and an external benchmark, not a Metaphilosophy dataset.

## Decision

The five prominent public examples in the LMCA paper are useful for methodological explanation but unsuitable as Metaphilosophy calibration or production items.

All five are excluded because their expert ratings are already visible, their intended methodological lessons are discussed in the paper, and direct LMCA row reuse or redistribution has not been approved. Using them as qualification items would partly test whether a participant remembers or infers the published answer rather than whether the participant can independently apply the rubric.

No position text, critique text, or human rating value is copied into the machine-readable screening record. Advisers may receive links to the public paper and table identifiers only.

## Coverage obtained from the source examples

The five examples jointly illustrate:

- interpretation and target ambiguity;
- priced-in objection ambiguity;
- strength–centrality allocation ambiguity;
- correctness versus strength;
- intuition-pump and bottom-line disagreement;
- preservation of residual disagreement; and
- superficial model agreement.

They do not adequately provide fresh, label-hidden examples of:

- genuinely low clarity near or below the 0.5 branch;
- practical verification of a correctness-sensitive factual or logical claim;
- dead weight versus an unsuccessful substantive argument;
- single issue versus multiple independent issues; or
- an item whose expected evaluation has not already been disclosed.
