### Q-006A — approve the consultation design (approved)

The following non-binding consultation and screening design is approved:

1. **Pilot structure:** 12 positions × 4 critiques = 48 critiques.
2. **Independent review:** 2 blind initial ratings per critique = 96 initial ratings.
3. **Assignment unit:** all four sibling critiques stay with the same pair of raters.
4. **Panel:** 6 early-career core raters and 2 dedicated adjudicators.
5. **Topic proposal:** 2 positions in each of six families:
   - normative ethics;
   - political philosophy;
   - epistemology and philosophy of science;
   - philosophy of mind and AI consciousness;
   - decision theory and social choice; and
   - metaphilosophy and AI governance.
6. **Eligible item sources:** public synthetic material receiving new expert ratings and protected public-domain-derived positions that pass source-fidelity and ambiguity/scope review.
7. **Preferred source crossing:** target 6 positions from each source class, with one position from each source class in every topic family. If screening makes this infeasible, retain at least 4 from each source class and document every deviation for Q-006B.
8. **Balanced anonymous assignment:** 12 distinct rater pairs; four positions, four distinct partners, four distinct topic families, and—under the preferred crossing—two positions from each source class per core rater.
9. **Assignment eligibility and failure rule:** every assigned topic family must appear in the participant's separately approved coverage record. Authorship conflict and prior exposure are hard exclusions. If no participant-to-slot mapping satisfies competence, conflict, exposure, and balance, generate no assignment rather than relaxing a constraint or making an undocumented swap.
10. **Candidate acquisition:** screen at least 8 critique candidates per position before provisionally selecting 4, while preserving frozen acquisition-judge, length, formatting, citation, source, attack-family, disagreement, and source-style-cue diagnostics.
11. **Rubric:** centrality, strength, correctness, clarity, dead weight, single issue, and overall; analyze substantive impact through `strength × centrality`.
12. **Blindness:** hide source class and identity, author/model identity, acquisition-judge outputs, provisional strata, paired-rater identity and ratings, aggregate ratings, labels, and adjudication status until initial ratings lock.
13. **Blind task-packet proposal:** after later controlled approvals, give each core rater one participant-specific packet containing four positions and four sibling critiques per position. Replace controlled item IDs with opaque participant-specific task tokens; bind the packet to the assignment, manifest, rubric, exact text versions, blindness declarations, and response schema; retain a private operator index; and require all sixteen assigned responses exactly once under the packet hash.
14. **Rating-ingestion proposal:** structural submission validity does not imply acceptance. Canonically hash submissions, require one quality-control disposition and reason per response, materialize only accepted responses, retain rejected raw submissions and decisions separately, use explicit no-ops for already-materialized responses, reject replay and duplicate initials, and bind every batch to a private ingestion event and before/after dataset hashes.
15. **Artifact separation:** participant selection does not authorize assignment; assignment does not authorize task-packet generation; packet generation does not authorize distribution; distribution does not authorize rating; validation does not authorize acceptance; acceptance does not authorize ingestion; ingestion does not authorize adjudication; and case generation does not authorize distribution, adjudication work, rerating, closure acceptance, snapshot sign-off, or payment.
16. **Shared calibration proposal:** all six core raters and both adjudicators independently rate the same 8 public, non-protected critiques across 2 positions. The exact examples, considerations dossier, remediation rule, and qualification threshold remain pending Q-006B.
17. **Review candidates:** item review when either rater assigns clarity below 0.5; evidence review when either reports an unresolved correctness-sensitive verification issue.
18. **Small-sample safeguards:** publish position-level results; use position as the resampling unit; report leave-one-position-out sensitivity; separate pre- and post-adjudication results; treat reliability coefficients, model comparisons, and subgroup effects as exploratory.
19. **Adjudication proposal:** open one critique-level case only from explicitly approved operative routes; preserve both initial ratings; assign across two topic-qualified, conflict-screened adjudicators; allow closure without rerating, after a valid original-rater append-only rerating, or explicitly unresolved; prohibit imposed consensus and replacement scores; require both adjudicators to sign a distribution-preserving final snapshot.
20. **Senior role:** bounded methodological review only—approximately 20 asynchronous minutes or one 30-minute call; no bulk rating, routine adjudication, continuing board role, or implied endorsement.

## Source-grounded rationale

The supplied LMCA paper reports 951 rated critiques and a seven-dimensional rubric. It hides source and tags during rating, generally presents sibling critiques together, preserves initial ratings when object-level discussion leads to revision, identifies interpretation as a major source of disagreement, and notes source/style confounding, rater concentration, and insufficient within-position quality spread. It also treats `strength × centrality` as the relevant combined quantity and uses only clarity and overall when human clarity is below 0.5.

Metaphilosophy's deterministic assignment, opaque task tokens, packet commitments, replay-safe ingestion, controlled adjudication, and distribution-preserving final snapshot are project-specific operational extensions. They should be reviewed as such rather than attributed to LMCA.

## Provisional adviser questions

### Numerical adjudication candidates

- overall-score difference ≥ 0.30;
- `strength × centrality` difference ≥ 0.30;
- correctness difference ≥ 0.35;
- clarity difference ≥ 0.35;
- item review whenever either rater scores clarity below 0.5;
- evidence review whenever either rater reports unresolved correctness-sensitive verification; and
- review whenever either rater flags insufficient context, source fidelity, ambiguity, scope, or leakage.

Advisers should assess whether these triggers are redundant, too permissive, too restrictive, or likely to create selection or capacity bias. Non-numeric routes should be reviewed as item/evidence routes rather than mechanisms for pressuring agreement.

### Scale-readiness candidates

- median accepted-rating time ≤ 15 minutes;
- weighted within-position ordering agreement ≥ 0.75 for critique pairs whose mean overall gap is at least 0.20;
- at least 9 of 12 positions with a latest-accepted overall-score spread ≥ 0.30; and
- no more than 25% of critiques unresolved because of defective context, source fidelity, ambiguity, or scope.

These remain diagnostic candidates. Meeting them would not automatically activate Phase 2.
