## D1 — blind self-check scope

**Selected: B.** Use a balanced 24-rating blind self-check subsample: six positions, one per topic family and three per source class; two critiques per selected position; both original raters self-check; four self-check records per core rater. Selection is frozen before any pilot rating and is independent of observed human scores or disagreement.

## D2 — interpretation-cause coding coverage

**Selected: A.** Two dedicated adjudicators independently code all 48 paired interpretation fingerprints before either sees numeric scores or gaps, the other adjudicator's code, model judgments, aggregate results, or adjudication outcomes. Both initial codes remain immutable; later reconciliation may preserve agreement, disagreement, or an unresolved classification without overwriting either code.

## Boundary

These decisions approve the endpoint design only. They do not modify the current workflow, approve Q-006B, freeze protected items or thresholds, authorize participant access, recruitment, research start, payment, merge, or deployment. The adjudicator workload and honorarium plan must be re-estimated before named commitments because D2 expands cause coding to all 48 pairs.
