# Decision: auditable expert judgment before a general leaderboard

- **Decision status:** accepted
- **Approved by:** Ellen Sun
- **Approval date:** 15 August 2026
- **Pre-approval credence recorded in the decision proposal:** 0.88
- **Strategy contract:** `ops/research-positioning-v1.json`
- **Human-readable positioning:** `docs/research-positioning-v1.md`

Metaphilosophy's primary direction is to build and study an auditable expert-judgment system for philosophical and conceptual reasoning. Expert disagreement, interpretation differences, checking, revision, and judge robustness are the main research objects.

An ordinary LMCA-style model leaderboard is explicitly excluded as the project's current core direction. Model evaluation may be added only as a secondary diagnostic after the human-label foundation has been assessed.

The planned 48-critique study remains a measurement and workflow feasibility pilot. This decision does not freeze its quantitative estimands, aggregation rules, uncertainty intervals, thresholds, or sample-size adequacy. Those require a separate approved design amendment before research use.

This decision does not modify public copy, authorize recruitment or participant access, start research, merge a pull request, or deploy production.
