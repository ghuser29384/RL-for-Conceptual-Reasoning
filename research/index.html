Can expert judgment become reliable evidence for AI philosophy?

This pilot is designed to test a workflow, not to manufacture a philosophical answer key. Qualified reviewers would independently evaluate contextualized critiques, preserve disagreement, and produce an auditable record of what was rated, why it changed, and what remains uncertain.

02 · Pilot design

A small test with deliberately hard constraints.

The pilot asks whether a balanced, blind, multi-rater process is feasible before Metaphilosophy attempts a larger expert-rated programme.

01

Contextualized positions

Twelve positions are proposed across six topic families. Each receives four sibling critiques so reviewers can make within-position comparisons.

02

Independent initial judgment

Every critique receives two blind initial ratings. The same rater pair sees all four siblings, while source, author/model identity, provisional strata, and other ratings remain hidden.

03

Balanced assignment

Six core raters are mapped onto twelve distinct pairs. Topic coverage, conflicts, prior exposure, consent, calibration, and availability are hard constraints; an infeasible roster produces no assignment.

04

Separate adjudication

Two dedicated adjudicators may review only cases opened by a later approved policy. They cannot replace, average, or impose human scores.

05

Append-only evidence

Initial ratings are preserved. A later object-level reconsideration creates a predecessor-linked record rather than overwriting the original judgment.

06

Position-level reporting

Primary diagnostics remain position-level with leave-one-position-out sensitivity. Reliability, topic, source, model, and subgroup findings are exploratory.

03 · Rating rubric

Evaluate the critique—not agreement with its conclusion.

The proposed seven dimensions follow the LMCA rubric. Scores run from zero to one, but the definitions—not intuitive labels alone—govern their meaning.

  1. 01

    Centrality

    How much would the position weaken if the claims attacked by the critique were successfully refuted?

  2. 02

    Strength

    How successfully does the critique undermine the particular claims or inferences it attacks?

  3. 03

    Correctness

    What proportion of the critique is substantively correct rather than false or misleading?

  4. 04

    Clarity

    After careful reading, can the critique’s intended meaning and implications be pinned down?

  5. 05

    Dead weight

    How much content fails to contribute to the critique’s argument, clarification, or evidential value?

  6. 06

    Single issue

    Does the critique focus on one line of attack rather than several independent objections?

  7. 07

    Overall

    How good is the critique all considered—its force, insight, precision, correctness, and economy?

Two source-derived rules

Strength and centrality should be interpreted through their product when measuring substantive impact. When human clarity is below 0.5, non-clarity dimensions may be unreliable, so the custom discrepancy summary retains clarity and overall only.

04 · Safeguards

Designed to fail closed rather than look complete.

Artifact creation and permission to use that artifact are separate. A generated assignment, packet, submission, case, or snapshot never silently authorizes the next stage.

Source and style confounds

Candidate selection records length, formatting, citations, source, author/model identity, attack family, acquisition-judge disagreement, and obvious source-style cues before ratings begin.

Procedural blindness

Rater-facing packets omit source class and identity, author/model identity, acquisition-judge outputs, provisional quality strata, paired-rater information, aggregate ratings, labels, and adjudication status.

Submission integrity

Participant-specific task tokens and cryptographic commitments bind each submission to the exact assignment, rubric, context, text versions, and response schema the reviewer saw.

Quality control without rewriting

Each submitted response is explicitly accepted, rejected without materialization, or treated as an already-materialized no-op. Quality control cannot silently edit the reviewer’s score content.

Disagreement preservation

Review may close without rerating, after a valid append-only rerating, or explicitly unresolved. No adjudicator may create a consensus score or declare a winning rater.

No automatic expansion

Even favorable pilot results do not activate the 400-critique programme. Expansion separately requires methodological review, sufficient funding or committed qualified capacity, and a new owner decision.

06 · Claim discipline

What this pilot cannot establish.

  • It cannot show that 48 critiques are sufficient to train a materially better model.
  • It cannot settle philosophical questions or create an objective philosophical ground truth.
  • It cannot support public rankings of individual raters from sixteen production ratings each.
  • It cannot establish causal superiority of a topic, source class, model family, or rater type.
  • It cannot make LMCA ratings, public synthetic critiques, and new Metaphilosophy ratings interchangeable.
  • It cannot authorize the 400-critique Phase 2 programme automatically.

Public review

The protocol is visible before the results exist.

That is deliberate. Methodological criticism is more valuable before items, participants, thresholds, and claims become difficult to change.
