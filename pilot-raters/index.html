Early-career expert-rater pilot

Help test how expert judgments of philosophical critiques can be made reliable.

Pilot 01 will collect two independent blind ratings for each of 48 public critiques. We are seeking advanced philosophy doctoral students, postdocs, recent PhDs, and directly relevant early-career researchers.

48 public critiques 96 required initial ratings 12–24 likely ratings per selected rater $400 pooled core-rater honoraria

The honorarium is a capped pool distributed proportionally by accepted rating units. It is not a wage or a guarantee of any individual amount. This page collects interest only; payment and tax information are not requested here.

What selected raters do

  1. Complete a separate public calibration without external AI assistance.
  2. Rate a blind tranche using the seven LMCA dimensions and Rubric v2.
  3. Respond to narrowly triggered clarification or re-rating requests.
  4. Provide short feedback on the instructions and workflow.

This is a development pilot, not a hidden benchmark. All 48 critiques come from the public synthetic library and are currently unrated. Pilot results will be used to revise the method and estimate the resources needed for a future protected Hard Set.

Frequently asked questions

Does applying commit me to the full pilot?

No. This is an expression of interest. Selected applicants receive an exact proposed assignment range and dates before committing.

Is this a paid job?

No. The current USD 400 core-rater budget is a limited pooled volunteer honorarium distributed by accepted units. It is not a wage or full-cost payment, and no individual amount is guaranteed.

Are the items confidential?

No. Pilot 01 uses public synthetic development material. Protected future Hard Set material is not used in this pilot or shown during recruitment.

Will I need to provide tax or banking information here?

No. This form does not request sensitive payment or tax data. Activated contributors complete any required onboarding later through an appropriate process.

How are raters selected?

A human reviewer verifies relevant training or equivalent experience, reviews conflicts and availability, and evaluates a separate calibration. Prestige and affiliation alone do not qualify an applicant.
